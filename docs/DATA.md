# Data sources and partitions

The artifact includes derived paper records, original-index or name mappings where recovered, and published layout and Validation source data. Obtain source corpora through their providers. The archive includes no copies of raw third-party corpora.

| Domain | Upstream source | In-package construction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ENZYMES, PROTEINS, IMDB-BINARY | [TUDataset collections](https://chrsmrrs.github.io/datasets/docs/datasets/) | `deepgd/datasets/tu_dataset.py` and `assets/*_index.txt` |
| SBM | Generated by the project's stochastic block model loader | `deepgd/datasets/sbm_dataset.py`, `assets/sbm_index.txt` |
| WN18RR and FB15K237 | Sampled subgraphs of training triples from the two source collections | `deepgd/datasets/kg_sampled_dataset.py` gives the exact downloader endpoints and generation options |
| NORTH | [Graph Drawing data repository](https://graphdrawing.unipg.it/data.html) | `deepgd/datasets/north_dataset.py`, `assets/north_index.txt` |
| ROME | [Graph Drawing data repository](https://graphdrawing.unipg.it/data.html) | `deepgd/datasets/rome_dataset.py`, `assets/rome_index.txt` |

Use the exact index, seed, and split mode from the relevant archived command. The named corpora are inputs to graph layout evaluation, with filtering and conversion in the loaders. The original download dates and exact versions are not available for all datasets. `reference/05_DATASET_PROVENANCE.csv` provides the saved graph counts, hash links and limits. `results/hctf_graph_metadata.csv` and `reference/evidence/claim_graph_identity_index.csv` map retained observations to available identities.

## Cohorts

- **Eight-domain shared warm-start.** 120 run tables from 8 domains, 5 seeds and 3 variants. Each variant contributes 9,725 rows, with 4,830 distinct domain-graph identities. Of 40 downstream splits, 35 warm membership maps were recovered. Of those, 28 have warm training overlap and the remaining 7 have warm test overlap. Five ROME mappings remain unresolved.
- **Strict size extrapolation.** Original training-evaluation records are under `results/hctf_strict_size_ood/`. The Q1 reevaluation is under `results/hctf_q1_controls/`. Each uses 3 domains and 3 seeds. Use the matching archive and confidence intervals.
- **GraphLocal-v1 and N×F×C.** In-package graphwise CSV and coordinate arrays are Validation outputs. The independent graphwise Test inputs and outputs are outside the published manuscript artifact.
- **Metric dictionaries.** The paired completion markers enumerate the ten in-selection metrics and seven post-selection metrics. Selection records store `selected_alpha`, `accepted`, `selected_source` and `score`. The join key within a domain/seed/variant is `batch_id,graph_id`. Main study files instead use `batch_id,local_graph_id`.
- **Layout examples.** Figure 1 stage files and Figure 10 layout CSVs preserve archived examples. Figure 2 encodes training objectives schematically. Figure 3 uses a fixed small forest construction example. Layout display is governed by the original plotting scripts.
